# Contributing

Improvements are welcome, especially from real use.

- **Found a bad recommendation, a missing question or a weak source?** Open an issue with the sport, what went wrong and what you expected. Remove any personal data first.
- **Adding a sport:** add its questions under "Sport-specific add-ons" in `references/interview.md`, and its key sizing rules with a source link.
- **Changing the method:** keep the three phases (interview, research, report) and the rules in `SKILL.md`: nothing from memory, 2 independent sources per model, no purchases or logins, no medical advice.
- Add a line to `CHANGELOG.md`, and to `LESSONS.md` if the change comes from a real use.
