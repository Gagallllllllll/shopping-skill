# Contributing

Improvements are welcome, especially from real use.

- **Found a bad recommendation, a missing question or a weak source?** Open an issue with the category, what went wrong and what you expected. Remove any personal data first.
- **Adding or improving a category pack:** copy the structure of an existing file in `references/categories/` (interview add-ons, target specs, trusted sources, price and timing, second-hand checks, legal and safety, traps). Give a source link for every rule or fact, and mark what you could not verify as "not confirmed". Add the pack to the table in `SKILL.md`.
- **Changing the method:** keep the phases (interview, research, best price, report and buying sheets, self-improvement) and the rules in `SKILL.md`: nothing from memory, 2 independent sources per model, delivered prices, no purchases or logins, no medical or financial advice.
- Add a line to `CHANGELOG.md`, and to `LESSONS.md` if the change comes from a real use.
