# Interview guide and client profile

Load the category pack first (`references/categories/<category>.md`): its questions come on top of these. Pick the questions that change the recommendation, skip anything already answered, and give a default or examples with every question.

## Question bank (every category)

**The need**
- What do you want to buy (one item, a set, an upgrade)? Why now: first purchase, broken or worn, want better, new use?
- What frustrates you about what you have today, or what should the new one do better?
- Any model or brand you already like, have tried or want to avoid? Why?

**Use**
- How, where and how often will you use it? For whom (you, family, a gift)?
- Your level or experience with this kind of product, if it matters (category pack says).
- Must-haves and nice-to-haves, as a short list.

**Fit and constraints**
- Sizes or measurements the category needs (the pack lists them: body measurements, room dimensions, device compatibility...).
- Space, transport, storage, compatibility with what you already own.
- Physical constraints if relevant (general information only, never a diagnosis).

**Budget and buying**
- Budget, and what it covers. Are you flexible if a better match costs 10 to 20% more?
- New only, or open to previous models, outlet, refurbished or second-hand?
- **Delivery country (always ask: it changes every total through shipping, VAT and customs) and the countries or sites you accept to buy from.** Online, shop, or try before buying?
- When do you need it?

**Taste and values**
- Look, colours, style. Brands you trust.
- Durability, repairability, eco-design, made in Europe: does any of this matter?

**Search depth (ask last)**
- How deep should the search go: Quick, Standard (default) or Deep? Deep takes longer and costs more, but covers every second-hand site and more shops.

## Client profile template

```
CLIENT PROFILE: <name or nickname> · <category>

Need: <what, why now>
Use: <how, where, how often, level if relevant>
Fit and constraints: <sizes, measurements, compatibility, space>
Budget: <amount, scope, new / refurbished / second-hand, delivery country>
Success looks like: <what the client wants to feel or achieve>
Deal-breakers: <hard nos>
Search depth: <Quick / Standard / Deep>
Assumed (not confirmed by the client): <defaults used>

TARGET SPECS (derived, with reasoning)
- <spec>: <value> because <reason, source of the rule>
- ...
```
