## Interview add-ons (default in brackets)

**All devices**
- Ecosystem already owned (Apple, Android, Windows, camera lens mount)? [keep it]
- How many years should it last? [4 to 5]
- New, refurbished by a professional, or second-hand? [new or pro refurbished with at least 12 months warranty]
- Trade-in or resale of the old device? [no]
- Country of use (warranty, plugs, mobile bands, TV tuner, keyboard layout)? [buyer's country]

**Laptop:** main use (office and web, studies, photo or video, development, gaming, 3D) [office and web]; portable under 1.5 kg or desk-bound [portable]; OS [current one]; screen size and quality needs [14 inch]; specific software (CAD, Adobe, Windows-only) [none]; keyboard layout (AZERTY, QWERTZ, Swiss) [country default].

**Phone:** iOS or Android [current]; camera priority (low light, zoom, video) [medium]; battery 1 or 2 days [1.5]; size and one-hand use [6.1 to 6.4 inch]; years of use [5]; eSIM or dual SIM [yes]; storage use [normal].

**Tablet:** media, drawing and notes, work with keyboard, kids [media]; stylus and keyboard (extra cost) [no]; cellular or Wi-Fi [Wi-Fi]; size [11 inch].

**TV:** viewing distance and room light [3 m, mixed]; use (films, sport, console gaming, streaming) [films and streaming]; size limit (stand, wall, furniture width) [55 inch]; sound (soundbar planned?) [plan one]; tuner needs for the country [as per country].

**Audio:** context (commute, office, sport, home) [commute]; noise cancelling, wired or wireless [ANC, wireless]; phone and codecs [current phone]; fit (in-ear, over-ear, open) [in-ear]; call quality [important].

**Camera:** subjects (family, travel, sport and wildlife, portrait, video) [travel]; size and weight [compact]; lenses already owned [none]; photo only or video [photo plus some video]; level [beginner]; is a recent phone enough? [explain honestly].

## Turning needs into target specs

- **Laptop RAM:** 8 GB shows limits with modern apps; 16 GB is the standard recommendation; 32 GB for gaming, video editing, development. [V, retailer and maker guides, not neutral: hp.com, newegg.com]
- **Laptop storage:** 512 GB default, 256 GB only for light or cloud use. Check whether RAM and SSD are soldered or upgradeable. [NC]
- **Laptop battery:** use measured battery tests (Notebookcheck Wi-Fi test), not Wh or maker claims.
- **TV size:** viewing distance / 1.6 = diagonal for a 30 degree field of view (SMPTE-based); about 1.2 for a more cinematic 40 degrees. A 55 inch suits about 2.2 m, a 75 inch about 3 m. [V, rtings.com/tv/reviews/by-size/size-to-distance-relationship]
- **TV panel:** OLED for dark rooms, Mini-LED or QLED for bright rooms; console gaming needs HDMI 2.1, 120 Hz, VRR. Check each model's lab test. [NC]
- **Phone and tablet support (EU):** for models placed on the market from 20 June 2025, OS updates for at least 5 years after the last unit is sold, spare parts for 7 years, batteries rated at least 800 cycles at 80% capacity. [V via the European Commission news page; re-check the regulation text]
- **Phone storage:** 128 GB light use, 256 GB default with photos and video, 512 GB and more for heavy video. [NC]
- **Camera:** sensor size trades size against low-light quality; always count lenses in the budget. [NC]

## Trusted sources

- **Lab tests and reviewers:** RTINGS (TVs, monitors, headphones, soundbars, laptops), Notebookcheck (laptops, phones), GSMArena (phone specs, battery tests, regional variants), DXOMARK (one input among others), iFixit (repairability). Paywall status [NC].
- **Consumer testers:** UFC-Que Choisir and 60 Millions de consommateurs (France), Stiftung Warentest (Germany), K-Tipp and Kassensturz (Switzerland). Mostly partly paywalled [NC].
- **Official labels (EU):** energy label for TVs and displays, and since 20 June 2025 for phones and tablets (energy class, battery endurance, repairability class A to E, dust, water and drop resistance); models are listed in the EPREL database (eprel.ec.europa.eu, scan the QR code on the label). [V summary; EPREL page not opened]
- **France:** repairability index ("indice de réparabilité") still covers laptops among other categories; smartphones were removed (now covered by the EU label); TVs moved to the durability index ("indice de durabilité") from 8 January 2025. [V, ecologie.gouv.fr/politiques-publiques/indice-reparabilite]
- Prefer sources that publish their method; affiliate "best of" lists are weak sources.

## Price and timing

- Black Friday 27 Nov 2026, Cyber Monday 30 Nov 2026. [V, secondary source]

## Second-hand checks

- **Battery:** iPhone: Settings > Battery > Battery Health; 80% or more is within Apple's normal range; below that, negotiate or plan a replacement. [V, support.apple.com/en-gb/104999] Laptops: cycle count (macOS System Information, Windows `powercfg /batteryreport`) [NC]. Android: no standard menu [NC].
- **Activation lock:** Apple advises not to buy an iPhone with Activation Lock on: meet the seller, have them sign out of their Apple Account and erase the device in front of you. [V] Android: ask the seller to remove the Google account and reset (Factory Reset Protection) [NC].
- **IMEI:** dial *#06# and check the blacklist with a free IMEI checker; a reset does not remove a blacklist; a clean result does not prove the phone is unlocked or paid off. [V, imeicheck.com] Also check it is not company-managed (MDM) [NC].
- **Screen and functions:** OLED burn-in (full grey or white screen), dead pixels, touch dead zones, Face ID or fingerprint, "non-genuine part" messages.
- **Warranty:** a professional seller owes the legal guarantee; a private seller does not. Keep the invoice. A maker's commercial warranty may not transfer [NC].
- **Scam signs:** price far below market, payment outside the platform, fake escrow or courier emails, photos that don't match, "iCloud will be removed later".

## Legal and safety

- **EU legal guarantee:** at least 2 years from delivery; defects in the first year presumed present at delivery (some countries 2 years); for second-hand from a professional it can be shortened but not below 1 year; private sales not covered. [V, europa.eu/youreurope ... guarantees-returns]
- **EU 14-day withdrawal** for online purchases, from delivery; the buyer pays return postage unless the seller offers it or did not inform; exceptions include unsealed media and made-to-order goods. [V, europa.eu/youreurope ... returns]
- **Switzerland:** no general 14-day withdrawal for online purchases (shops may offer returns voluntarily); 2-year warranty for new goods, can be reduced to 1 year for second-hand from a professional; report defects promptly. [V on konsum.admin.ch FAQ; legal text not read]
- **Recalls:** EU Safety Gate (ec.europa.eu/safety-gate), France RappelConso (rappel.conso.gouv.fr). [V] Swiss recall list [NC].

## Traps

- "Up to" speeds and battery claims, peak versus sustained performance, "HDR" without brightness, TV "motion rate", megapixels versus sensor size, gaming laptops with a low-power GPU.
- Regional variants: same name, different chip, mobile bands, SIM tray, charging speed, TV tuner, keyboard. Check the exact model number.
- Chargers often not in the box; USB-C common charger rule in the EU [NC: verify scope].
- Grey imports and private marketplace sellers: no local warranty, wrong plug, no legal guarantee.
- Warranty limited to the region of purchase (some cameras and brands) [NC].
- Extended warranties that duplicate the legal guarantee; "refurbished" without a defined grade.
