# Category pack: fashion and shoes

Clothing, outerwear, everyday shoes and sneakers, bags. Technical sports gear: `sport.md`. Status: **draft** (researched 2026-11-16, not yet used on a real case). Marks: **[V]** verified on an official page during research, **[S]** secondary source, **[NC]** not confirmed: re-check before stating it to a client. ## Interview add-ons (default in brackets)

- Item and occasions: what, where, how often worn? [everyday, 3 to 4 days a week]
- Style and dress code; colours already owned and what it must match? [neutral, versatile]
- **Measurements in cm.** Tops, jackets, dresses: height, chest at the fullest point, waist at the navel, hips at the widest, shoulder width, sleeve length. Trousers: waist, hips, inseam, rise. Shoes: the longer foot's length (measured in the evening) and width across the ball. Also: current sizes in which brands, and what fits well or badly. No tape measure: measure a well-fitting garment laid flat.
- Fit: slim, regular or loose; layering underneath? [regular]
- Shoes: foot shape (wide, narrow, high instep), orthotics, hours on foot per day, surfaces, weather. [cushioned, 8 hours]
- Materials to avoid: itchy wool, allergies (nickel, latex, chromium in leather), no leather, synthetics, water-repellent finishes with PFAS. [none]
- Care: machine wash only, no dry cleaning, no ironing; resoling acceptable? [30 degree machine wash]
- Budget per item, with cost per wear for items worn often; delivery and returns included? [ask for a range]
- Ethics: second-hand, made in Europe, transparency, repairability, certifications. [second-hand ok]
- Wardrobe gap: what it replaces, what it must pair with.

## Turning needs into target specs

**Sizing**
- Use the brand's own chart in cm, never a generic table: clothing sizes are not standardised and differ by brand and country. [S] The EU standard EN 13402 (sizes from body measurements in cm) is voluntary. [NC]
- Check whether a chart gives body or garment (flat) measurements; compare garment measurements with a well-fitting piece. Between sizes: larger for woven fabrics, smaller for stretch.
- Reviews: look for "runs small", "true to size", and reviewers who give height and weight; read 1 to 3 star reviews first and look for patterns.
- **Shoes, length:** heel against a wall on paper, mark the longest toe, measure in mm, both feet, in the evening; use the longer foot. [S]
- **Shoes, width:** across the ball of the foot. Mondopoint (ISO 9407) gives length in mm, sometimes with width (e.g. 270/100). [S]
- **Shoe systems:** an EU size step is 2/3 cm (Paris point); the allowance added to the foot varies by brand, so EU 43 is roughly a 27.0 to 27.5 cm foot depending on the brand. US and UK scales differ by gender; width letters (A to E) are mainly used by US and UK brands. Always use the brand's chart in cm. [S]
- Sneakers can differ by half a size between models of the same brand: check the model's own chart and recent reviews.

**Quality and materials**
- **Fibre labels (EU):** the label must list fibre names from the official list with percentages. [V, Regulation (EU) No 1007/2011] Footwear follows a separate directive (materials of upper, lining and sole, with pictograms). [V, European Commission FAQ]
- Fibre rules of thumb [NC]: natural fibres or blends with 5 to 20% synthetic for durability; high elastane (above about 5%) wears out sooner; acrylic pills.
- **Weight (GSM)** measures thickness and warmth, not quality. Merino knits: light 150 to 180, mid 200 to 250, heavy 300 and more. [S] Denim: oz x 33.9 = GSM; under 12 oz light, 12 to 16 mid, over 16 heavy; everyday jeans 10 to 13 oz. [S]
- **Construction:** stitch density (about 10 per 2.5 cm), flat even seams, matched patterns at seams, secure buttons and spares, generous seam allowances, reinforced stress points.
- **Shoe construction:** Goodyear welt is the easiest to resole; Blake stitch is slimmer and resoleable by specialists; cemented (glued) soles are light and cheap but in practice not resoleable. [S]
- **Leather:** full-grain and top-grain are the better grades; "bonded" leather is leather fibre glued with a binder; "vegan leather" is usually polyurethane or PVC and cracks earlier. [NC]

## Trusted sources

- Brand size guides and product pages; retailer charts for multi-brand shops.
- Quality and fit communities and guides (for example r/malefashionadvice, r/femalefashionadvice, r/goodyearwelt; specialist guides on shoes and denim). Vet each source. [NC]
- Consumer testers: UFC-Que Choisir, 60 Millions de consommateurs (France); FRC, Stiftung für Konsumentenschutz (Switzerland); Stiftung Warentest (Germany). [NC]
- **Certifications:** OEKO-TEX Standard 100 tests the finished product for harmful substances; it says nothing about organic fibre (organic has its own OEKO-TEX label). [NC] GOTS certifies the whole chain for organic textiles: "organic" needs at least 95% certified organic fibre, "made with organic materials" at least 70%. [S: verify on global-standard.org] Check any label's certificate number on the issuer's database.
- **France, environmental cost for textiles:** a framework set by decree 2025-957 with the Ecobalyse calculator, voluntary since 1 October 2025; scope 11 clothing categories, footwear excluded for now. [S: verify on legifrance.gouv.fr and ecobalyse.beta.gouv.fr]

## Price and timing

- **France, soldes:** two 4-week periods a year. Winter sales start on the second Wednesday of January (first Wednesday if the second falls after 12 January); summer sales on the last Wednesday of June (second to last if the last falls after 28 June). 2027, mainland France: 6 January to 2 February, and 23 June to 20 July. Some departments have other dates. [S, citing service-public.fr: verify there]
- Outside soldes: promotions, private sales, Black Friday (27 Nov 2026). An announced reduction in the EU must refer to the lowest price of the previous 30 days. [NC]
- **Switzerland:** no state-regulated sale periods; shops set their own. [NC]
- End of season: coats and boots drop in February and March, summer items in August; sneakers drop when new colourways arrive.
- Outlets often sell last season or lower-quality made-for-outlet lines: check labels and item codes. [NC]
- **Second-hand platforms:** Vinted (verification service for selected items: 1 to 2 business days, refund if not authentic [S]), Vestiaire Collective (luxury, physical authentication [S]), Leboncoin, eBay, Ricardo and tutti (Switzerland).

## Second-hand checks

- Ask for flat measurements in cm (pit to pit, waist, length, shoulders, sleeves, inseam; insole length for shoes) and compare with a garment that fits.
- Photos: brand label and composition tag, care tag, inside seams, underarms, collar, crotch, elbows, soles and their wear pattern, in daylight.
- Pilling, thinning, shine on elbows and seat, stretched elastic, shrunk wool, yellowing, fading; odours (smoke, damp, perfume) are hard to remove: ask directly.
- Shoes: heel and sole wear, insole print, midsole cracks (old sneaker midsoles can crumble), smell.
- **Authenticity:** compare with the brand's own images, stitching, hardware engravings, labels, serial or date codes, receipts; for luxury, buy through a platform with authentication. A single check is not proof.
- **Scam signs:** payment outside the platform, moving to WhatsApp, "friends" payment links, fake payment or shipping pages, luxury far below market, stock photos, new accounts with no reviews.
- Buying from a private person: no 14-day withdrawal right; platform buyer protection applies instead. [V, europa.eu returns page]

## Legal and safety

- **EU 14-day withdrawal** for online purchases, from delivery, no reason needed; the buyer usually pays return postage unless the seller offers it or did not inform; defective goods: the trader pays. Exceptions include made-to-order items. [V, europa.eu/youreurope ... returns] Hygiene exceptions (unsealed underwear, swimwear) depend on national rules and the shop. [NC]
- **EU legal guarantee:** at least 2 years; a defect within the first year is presumed present at delivery (2 years in some countries). Wear and misuse are not defects. [V, europa.eu/youreurope ... guarantees-returns]
- **Switzerland:** no general withdrawal right for online purchases; returns depend on the shop's terms; faulty goods: repair, replacement, price reduction or cancellation. [S, SECO]
- **Recalls:** EU Safety Gate (includes clothing and shoes, for example cords on children's clothing, chromium VI in leather), France RappelConso. [V sites exist; not searched]
- Imports from outside the EU: VAT and possible duty on every parcel. [NC for 2026 changes]

## Traps

- Vanity sizing: the same size label differs by brand and decade; trust cm.
- Fast fashion: light fabrics, high polyester or elastane, thin linings; price is not proof of quality either, so check composition and construction.
- "Made in" claims are loosely regulated for clothing in the EU [NC]; "designed in" can hide the production country.
- Counterfeits: low prices, wrong fonts or logos, stock photos, "inspired by" listings.
- Return postage, especially cross-border and to Switzerland: check before buying.
- Greenwashing: "eco" or "conscious" collections without a certificate or measured score.
- Fake discounts: compare with price history.
- Stretch fabric hiding a tight fit, "one size" items, leather and wool that stretch with wear.
- Marketplace sellers claiming "ships from EU" without an EU address or returns.
