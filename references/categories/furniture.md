# Category pack: furniture and bedding

Sofas, mattresses, beds and bases, desks, office chairs, tables, storage. Status: **draft** (researched 2026-11-16, not yet used on a real case). Marks: **[V]** verified during research (official page or several agreeing sources), **[S]** secondary source, **[NC]** not confirmed: re-check before stating it to a client. Health questions: general information only, refer to a professional. Re-check legal points on official pages at the time of use.

## Interview add-ons (default in brackets)

1. Which piece(s), which room? [one piece, one room]
2. Room measurements: length, width, ceiling height, windows, radiators, sockets. [a tape-measure reading, never a guess]
3. **Access route** (the main cause of returns): clear door width and height, corridors and corners, stairs (width, turns, headroom), lift interior and door, floor, parking and carry distance. For a sofa: the narrowest point on the route. [assume nothing]
4. Users: number, height, rough weight band, age, mobility. Mattress: sleeping position (side, back, stomach, mixed), partner and weight difference, hot sleeper, allergies. Back or joint issues: general information only. [back sleeper, medium]
5. Use: daily or occasional, TV, naps, guest bed, home-office hours per day, dining for how many. [daily]
6. Style, colour, fabric versus leather versus microfibre, matching existing pieces. [neutral, washable cover]
7. Pets and children: claws, spills, removable covers, rounded corners, anti-tip fixings. [washable cover; rounded edges under 6 years]
8. Budget **including** delivery, assembly and removal of the old piece. [show 3 price tiers]
9. Delivery: to the room, assembly, take-back of the old piece, deadline, who receives it. [to the room]
10. Ethics and materials: FSC or PEFC wood, made in Europe, repairability, second-hand. [no strong preference; offer second-hand]
11. Where to buy: showroom test possible, online, second-hand (not for mattresses), country. [online ok]

## Turning needs into target specs

**Mattress firmness** (scale 1 to 10; general guidance, the client's own trial matters more) [V, sleepfoundation.org, a source with affiliate links]
- Side sleepers softer, back sleepers medium to firm, stomach sleepers firm.
- Under about 59 kg: medium-soft (around 4) for side and back sleepers.
- About 59 to 104 kg: medium (5) for side sleepers; medium-firm to firm (6 to 7) for back and stomach sleepers.
- Over about 104 kg: medium-firm to firm (6 to 8) on the side; firm to extra firm (7 to 9) on the back or stomach.
- Firmness scales are not standardised across brands. Persistent back pain: see a professional.
- Types [NC]: pocket springs (airy, durable), latex (resilient, ventilated), memory foam (pressure relief, warmer), hybrid. Foam density as a durability signal [NC: check testers].

**Sofa** [NC: general guidance]
- Seat height about 42 to 46 cm, seat depth about 55 to 60 cm (deeper for lounging, shallower for older users or short legs); test sitting.
- Frame: solid hardwood or plywood beats particle board; seat foam density and high-resilience foam as durability signals: ask the seller for figures.
- **Fabric abrasion (Martindale rub count):** under 10,000 decorative; 10,000 to 15,000 light domestic; 15,000 to 25,000 general domestic; 25,000 and more for heavy daily use, 30,000 and more with children or pets. It says nothing about pilling, fading or fire safety. [S, several retailer guides agree]

**Desk and office chair**
- Seated work surface adjustable between about 50 and 69 cm (20 to 27 inches), with clear knee and foot space under the desk; feet flat on the floor or on a footrest. [V, osha.gov computer workstations eTool]
- Fixed desks around 72 to 75 cm suit people of about 1.70 to 1.80 m; others need adjustability. [NC]
- Chair checklist: seat height, seat depth, lumbar support, recline with tension, armrest height, stable five-star base.
- **EN 1335** covers office work chairs: part 1 dimensions and types (by adjustment range), part 2 safety, part 3 test methods. A compliance claim should name the part and type. Exact type ranges: sources conflict [NC]. [V on the standard's structure]
- Standing desks: height range covering sitting and standing for the user, stability at full height, motor warranty, load capacity. [NC]

**Materials** [NC]
- Solid wood (one species throughout), veneer (thin real wood over a panel), particle board and MDF (fine for dry rooms and light loads, weak against water and repeated disassembly), plywood (strong). "Solid wood" often means the frame only: ask about tops, sides and back panel.
- Leather: full-grain, top-grain, split, bonded, "leather match" (leather only on contact surfaces): ask which surfaces are real leather.

## Trusted sources

- Consumer testers: UFC-Que Choisir and 60 Millions de consommateurs (France), Stiftung Warentest (Germany), K-Tipp, Saldo and FRC (Switzerland), Which? (UK). Coverage and paywalls [NC].
- International: Wirecutter, RTINGS (measured mattress tests: coverage [NC]), Sleep Foundation (guidance; rankings are commercial). Prefer measured lab tests; flag affiliate rankings.
- **Certifications:** CertiPUR (EU) and CertiPUR-US cover the polyurethane foam only, not springs, latex, fabric or the whole mattress [S]. OEKO-TEX Standard 100 tests textiles for harmful substances; "made with OEKO-TEX fabric" may cover only the cover [S]. FSC and PEFC certify forest sourcing of the wood, nothing about emissions of finishes or foams. Others to check on the issuer's site: NF Environnement Ameublement, Blue Angel, Greenguard, GOLS and GOTS, EU Ecolabel [NC].
- **France, eco-participation and take-back (Ecomaison):** an eco-participation is charged on new furniture and shown separately from the price [V, Ecomaison documents]. Free take-back by sellers since 2022: "1 for 1" at delivery or in store when buying an equivalent new item; "1 for 0" in stores over 1,000 m²; distance sellers above a turnover threshold must offer take-back. The old piece must be dismantled and accessible; ask when ordering. [V, Ecomaison seller memo] Individuals can also use recycling centres and Ecomaison collection points.

## Price and timing

- **France, soldes 2027:** winter 6 January to 2 February, summer 23 June to 20 July (mainland and online; some eastern departments differ). [S: several agreeing sources; verify on economie.gouv.fr]
- Black Friday 27 Nov 2026. Mattress "-50%" prices are often permanent: compare price history and the same model at several retailers; some brands rename models per retailer, which blocks comparison. [NC]
- **Mattress trials (100 nights etc.):** check who pays pickup, any fee, the minimum nights before a return (often 14 to 30), the required condition (protector, no stains), refund or exchange only, and whether the trial adds to the legal withdrawal right or replaces it. [NC: read the terms]
- Showroom: lie down 10 to 15 minutes in your usual position. Online: cheaper, but returning bulky goods is costly and awkward.
- Outlets and ex-display: ask about defects; the legal guarantee still applies to a professional sale; take photos.
- IKEA as-is and buy-back programmes: verify per country on ikea.com. [NC]
- **Second-hand:** Leboncoin (France), Ricardo and tutti (Switzerland), Facebook Marketplace, Selency (design and vintage), Emmaüs and ressourceries (France). Fees and protections for bulky goods [NC].

## Second-hand checks

- **Do not recommend a used mattress** (hygiene, bedbugs, wear). Upholstered pieces are riskier than hard furniture.
- **Bedbugs:** inspect mattress seams, zips, tufts, headboard back and edges, base slats, armchairs; signs are small black spots (droppings) on fabric, base or walls; slide a rigid card along seams; inspect in daylight with a torch. Steam treatment before bringing a piece in; wash textiles at 60°C or freeze them for 72 hours. Infested furniture must not be sold or left on the street. [V, French regional health agencies (ARS) and sante.gouv.fr]
- **Structure:** shake test for wobble, joints (dowels, screws, cam locks), glue gaps, cracks near joints, slats, sofa frame (lift one corner: it should not twist), seat sag, hardened foam, fabric wear at arms and seat edge, smoke or pet odour, missing hardware, whether it comes apart.
- **Pickup:** measure the piece and the route before agreeing; vehicle size, straps, helpers; pay at handover.
- **Scam signs:** payment links or QR codes sent by the seller, "paid courier" stories, advance payment before seeing the piece, price far below market, refusal to meet, fake payment screenshots, pressure ("another buyer is coming"). Never click links received in chat.

## Legal and delivery

- **EU 14-day withdrawal** for online purchases, from delivery; the seller refunds including standard delivery; the buyer pays return costs unless the seller offered or did not inform; made-to-measure and customised items are excluded; no withdrawal right when buying from a private person. [V, europa.eu/youreurope returns] Whether a seller can refuse a used mattress after unsealing: check the terms [NC].
- **EU legal guarantee:** at least 2 years for new goods [V]; can be reduced to 1 year for second-hand from a professional [NC: verify per country]. France also has the hidden-defects action (Civil Code) [S].
- **Delivery damage:** write precise reservations on the delivery note ("parcel opened, product damaged"), take photos before signing, refuse if clearly damaged, then confirm in writing within the carrier's deadline (often 3 working days in France [NC]).
- **Switzerland:** no general withdrawal right for online purchases; returns depend on the seller. Warranty 2 years for defects (art. 210 CO); report defects immediately; read the terms, some sellers limit remedies to repair. [V, seco.admin.ch and frc.ch]
- **Recalls:** France RappelConso (category furniture), EU Safety Gate. [V; recent furniture recalls include fire risks in recliners and wall cabinets with screws too short]

## Traps

- Pieces that do not fit through the door, stairs or lift: measure the route, check removable legs, arms and backs, keep a return option.
- "Free delivery" that stops at the kerb: ask for delivery to the room, assembly and removal of the old piece.
- Long lead times for made-to-order sofas: get a written date and the terms if late. [NC typical 8 to 16 weeks]
- "Solid wood" claims that only cover the frame.
- Trial periods confused with the legal withdrawal right.
- Eco-participation not shown separately in France; take-back not booked before delivery day.
- Fake discounts, and the same model sold under different names.
- Cheap sofas: particle-board frame, low-density foam, low rub count for daily use.
- New-foam smell: ventilate; check emission labels.
- Third-party marketplace sellers: guarantee and returns depend on the actual seller.
