# Interview guide and athlete profile

Pick the questions that change the recommendation. Skip anything already answered. Every question offers a default or examples.

## Question bank (all sports)

**The need**
- What do you want to buy (one item, the full kit, an upgrade)? Why now: new to the sport, broken or worn gear, want to progress, new terrain?
- What frustrates you about your current gear, or what do you want to feel that you don't feel today?
- Is there a model or brand you already like, have tried or want to avoid? Why?

**Level and practice**
- Level: beginner, intermediate, advanced, expert. How many years? Any lessons or coaching?
- Frequency: days or sessions per year, and how long each one lasts.
- Goals for the next 1 to 2 seasons: progress, comfort, performance, competition, fun with friends or family.
- Style or discipline, as a split: "out of 10 sessions, how many on X, Y, Z?"

**Body and fit**
- Height, weight, age bracket (optional), dominant side or stance if relevant.
- Sizes: shoe size and foot shape (wide, narrow, high instep), inseam and arm length for bikes, hand size for rackets and gloves.
- Physical constraints: past injuries, sensitive knees or back, flexibility (general information only, never a diagnosis).

**Conditions**
- Where you practise: countries, resorts, terrain, indoor or outdoor, altitude.
- Typical conditions: hard snow or powder, mud or road, cold or heat, wet or dry.
- Transport and storage: car, plane, public transport, small apartment.

**Budget and buying**
- Budget, and what it covers (one item or the full kit). Are you flexible if a better match costs 10 to 20% more?
- New only, or open to last season's models, outlet or second-hand?
- **Delivery country (always ask, it changes every total: shipping, VAT, customs) and countries or sites they accept to buy from.** Online, shop, or do you want to try before buying?
- When do you need it?

**Search depth (ask last)**
- How deep should the search go: Quick, Standard (default) or Deep? Deep takes longer and costs more, but covers every second-hand site and more shops.

**Taste and values**
- Look and colours, if they matter. Brands you trust.
- Durability, repairability, eco-design, made in Europe: does any of this matter to you?

## Sport-specific add-ons (examples, adapt to any sport)

- **Snowboard:** boot size and brand (compare with the current board's width: a wide board with medium boots feels slow edge to edge), stance (regular or goofy), current bindings, binding entry wanted (classic straps, quick entry with normal boots such as FASE or rear entry, or a step-in system that needs its own boots), preferred feel (playful or stable), carving or butters, speed.
- **Ski:** ski length today, turn style (short or long), boot flex and last width, touring or resort only.
- **Running:** weekly distance, race goals, pace, terrain (road, trail, track), pronation if known, current shoe and what you like about it.
- **Cycling:** road, gravel, MTB, commuting; distances and climbing; position (racy or comfortable); e-bike ok?
- **Climbing:** indoor or outdoor, bouldering or routes, foot shape, comfort or performance.
- **Surf:** wave type and size, current board volume, paddling fitness, session frequency.
- **Racket sports and golf:** playing style, injuries (tennis elbow), swing speed or handicap.
- **Fitness:** space at home, goals (strength, cardio), noise limits, budget per piece.
